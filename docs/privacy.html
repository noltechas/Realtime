Privacy Policy

Realtime Karaoke — last updated May 27, 2026

Realtime Karaoke is a private party karaoke app intended for personal use among friends. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and where it's stored.

What we collect

When you join a karaoke session, you may provide:

  • Display name — the name shown on the queue and stage.
  • Profile photo (optional) — an image you choose from your camera or photo library. Used as your avatar on the stage and in the queue.
  • Singer color (optional) — a color preference for your queue/stage display.
  • Session activity — songs you add to the queue, votes you cast, on-stage reactions and emojis you send, and song requests.

The app also requests permission to:

  • Camera — to scan the host's QR code to join a session.
  • Photo library / camera — to choose your profile picture. You can skip this entirely.

How we use it

Your name, photo, color, and activity are sent to the karaoke session you joined so the host's stage display and other participants can see your queue entries, votes, and reactions. Nothing else.

Where it's stored

Session data is stored on Supabase, a third-party database service used to coordinate sessions between your phone and the host's desktop app. Data is associated with a session code and the host's session. When a session ends, the associated data becomes inert and is not used for any further purpose.

Your profile photo and preferences may be stored locally on your device (in your browser's local storage or app local storage) so you don't have to re-enter them when rejoining the same session.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no third-party tracking, no analytics SDKs.
  • No account creation, no email or password collection.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information with anyone.
  • No use of your data outside the karaoke session you joined.

Children

Realtime Karaoke is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can leave a session at any time. To remove your participation data from a session, contact the host of that session — they can remove your queue entries and guest record from their session. To revoke camera or photo library access, change permissions in your phone's settings.
